# Application Views

This document maps the primary user interface views for Vidita Cafe.

## Frontend (Web PWA)

### 1. Discovery (Home)
- **Vertical Feed**: Paging vertical cards (Video, Insight, Suggestion).
- **Onboarding**: "Install Now" (PWA) and "Bounce" hint for scrolling.

### 2. Journey (Journal)
- **Journal List**: Calendar or list-based history of coffee experiences.
- **Entry View/Edit**: Detailed view of a specific brew (Coffee, Method, Rating, Tags).
- **Check-in UI**: Specific workflow for partner venue logging.

### 3. Brewing
- **Methods Grid**: Categorized by ownership (Ready to Brew vs. Explore).
- **Calculator**: Real-time scaling of dose/water.
- **Timer**: Full-screen immersive brewing guide.

### 4. Equipment
- **Inventory**: Management of owned gear.
- **Catalog**: Discovery of new gear with localized purchase links.

### 5. Search
- **Venue Directory**: Nearby specialty coffee shops and roasters.
- **Keyword Search**: Find venues by city, name, or style.

## Administration (Web)

### 1. Admin Dashboard
- **Global Stats**: User growth, journal volume, popular venues.
- **CMS**: Management of video content and affiliate links.
- **Venue Management**: Onboarding and managing partner venues.

### 2. Venue Manager
- **Venue Stats**: Detailed check-in analytics for specific shop owners.
- **Promotion Manager**: Create and manage local venue promotions.
