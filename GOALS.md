# Vidita Cafe - Application Goals

## 1. Learning Journey
Help users advance their coffee knowledge through interactive content.
- **Short Video Contents:** Curated bite-sized videos on coffee origins, processing, and techniques.
- **Quizzes & Interactive Activities:** Reinforce learning with engaging assessments.

## 2. Brewing Excellence
Assist users in executing various coffee brewing methods with precision.
- **Method Selection:** Support for V60, Chemex, Aeropress, French Press, Espresso, Moka Pot, Syphon, Cold Brew, etc.
- **Execution Parameters:** Adjustable settings for grind size, water temperature, and ratio.
- **Presets:** Ability to save and load favorite brewing configurations.
- **Guided Execution:** Real-time assistance with visible cues and integrated timers.

## 3. Journey Capture
Allow users to document and reflect on their personal coffee experiences.
- **Tasting Notes:** Record origin details, brewing methods, and sensory profiles (aroma, body, acidity).
- **Emotional Connection:** Log overall feelings and ratings for each cup.
- **History:** A searchable timeline of the user's coffee journey.

## 4. Equipment Management
Help users maintain their coffee tool library and discover new brewing possibilities.
- **Equipment Inventory:** Track owned grinders, brewers, scales, and kettles.
- **Requirement Verification:** Automatically check if the user has the necessary tools for a selected brewing method.
- **Smart Suggestions:** Prominently suggest missing equipment with guidance on what to acquire to unlock new methods.

## 5. Community & Local Discovery
Connect users with the best specialty coffee shops and roasters in their area and beyond.
- **Featured Coffee Shops:** Showcase partner and community-recommended cafés, with photos, descriptions, and location info.
- **Featured Roasters:** Highlight specialty roasters, their origin stories, and the beans they offer.
- **Local Discovery:** Help users find great coffee experiences near them, filtered by country or city.
- **Partner Platform:** Provide a channel for coffee shops and roasters to reach engaged, knowledgeable coffee enthusiasts.
