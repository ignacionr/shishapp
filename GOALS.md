# Shishapp - Application Goals

## 1. Learning Journey
Help users advance their shisha knowledge through interactive content.
- **Short Video Contents:** Curated bite-sized videos on tobacco varieties, packing techniques, and heat management.
- **Quizzes & Interactive Activities:** Reinforce learning with engaging assessments.

## 2. Session Excellence
Assist users in setting up various shisha configurations with precision.
- **Setup Selection:** Support for different bowl types (Phunnel, Vortex, Egyptian), HMDs, and tobacco brands.
- **Execution Parameters:** Adjustable settings for water level, charcoal count, and packing density.
- **Presets:** Ability to save and load favorite session configurations.
- **Guided Execution:** Real-time assistance with visible cues and integrated timers.

## 3. Session Capture
Allow users to document and reflect on their personal shisha experiences.
- **Tasting Notes:** Record tobacco brands, flavors, bowl types, and sensory profiles (cloud density, smoothness, flavor intensity).
- **Emotional Connection:** Log overall feelings and ratings for each session.
- **History:** A searchable timeline of the user's shisha journey.

## 4. Equipment Management
Help users maintain their shisha tool library and discover new setup possibilities.
- **Equipment Inventory:** Track owned hookahs, bowls, HMDs, and charcoal types.
- **Requirement Verification:** Automatically check if the user has the necessary tools for a specific setup.
- **Smart Suggestions:** Prominently suggest missing equipment with guidance on what to acquire to unlock new experiences.

## 5. Community & Local Discovery
Connect users with the best shisha lounges and tobacco shops in their area and beyond.
- **Featured Lounges:** Showcase partner and community-recommended lounges, with photos, descriptions, and location info.
- **Featured Brands:** Highlight premium tobacco brands and their flavor profiles.
- **Local Discovery:** Help users find great shisha experiences near them, filtered by country or city.
- **Partner Platform:** Provide a channel for lounges and brands to reach engaged shisha enthusiasts.
